# Oceans, rivers, mountains, deserts, and where animals live.
name: Nature
color: #3fa35b
order: 3

[easy]
Q: Which is the biggest ocean?
A: Pacific Ocean
X: Atlantic Ocean | Indian Ocean | Arctic Ocean
F: The Pacific covers almost a third of the whole Earth.

Q: Where do kangaroos live in the wild?
A: Australia
X: Africa | India | Brazil
F: A baby kangaroo is called a joey and rides in its mother's pouch.

Q: On which continent do emperor penguins live?
A: Antarctica
X: Europe | Asia | North America
F: Emperor penguin fathers keep the egg warm on their feet all winter.

Q: Where do giant pandas live in the wild?
A: China
X: Japan | India | Australia
F: A giant panda eats bamboo for about twelve hours a day.

Q: On which continent do giraffes live in the wild?
A: Africa
X: Asia | Australia | Europe
F: The giraffe is the tallest animal on Earth.

Q: What is the tallest mountain in the world?
A: Mount Everest
X: Mount Kilimanjaro | Mont Blanc | Mount Fuji
F: The top of Mount Everest is almost 9 kilometres above the sea.

Q: What do we call a very dry place where it hardly ever rains?
A: A desert
X: A jungle | A swamp | An island
F: Deserts can be hot or cold. What makes a desert is that it is dry.

Q: Which is the coldest continent?
A: Antarctica
X: Europe | Asia | Australia
F: Almost all of Antarctica is covered by ice, in places 4 kilometres thick.

Q: What do we call land with water all around it?
A: An island
X: A mountain | A valley | A desert
F: Some countries are islands, such as Iceland, Sri Lanka and Madagascar.

Q: Where do polar bears live?
A: The Arctic
X: Antarctica | Africa | Australia
F: Polar bears and penguins never meet: penguins live at the other end of the world.

Q: What do we call a mountain that can throw out hot lava?
A: A volcano
X: A canyon | A glacier | A cave
F: Lava is rock so hot that it has melted.

Q: What covers most of the Earth?
A: Water
X: Sand | Forest | Ice
F: Water covers about 71 parts in every 100 of the Earth.

Q: Which is the biggest hot desert in the world?
A: The Sahara
X: The Gobi | The Kalahari | The Mojave
F: The Sahara is almost as big as the whole United States.

Q: On which continent do tigers live in the wild?
A: Asia
X: Africa | South America | Europe
F: There are no wild tigers in Africa. Lions live there instead.

Q: On which continent is the Amazon rainforest?
A: South America
X: Africa | Asia | Australia
F: The Amazon is the biggest rainforest in the world.

Q: How many oceans are there?
A: Five
X: Three | Four | Seven
F: The Pacific, Atlantic, Indian, Southern and Arctic Oceans.

Q: What is the highest mountain in Africa?

F: Kilimanjaro is an old volcano in Tanzania with snow on top, near the equator.

Q: Where is the Great Barrier Reef?

F: It is the biggest thing on Earth built by living creatures: tiny corals.

Q: Which is the smallest ocean?

X: Indian Ocean | Southern Ocean | Atlantic Ocean
F: Much of the Arctic Ocean is covered by floating ice.

Q: On which island do lemurs live in the wild?
A: Madagascar
X: Sri Lanka | Iceland | Borneo
F: Lemurs live nowhere else in the wild.

Q: Which continent do llamas come from?
A: South America
X: Africa | Asia | Australia
F: Llamas carry loads in the Andes mountains.

Q: Which is the biggest island in the world?
A: Greenland
X: Madagascar | Iceland | Great Britain
F: Australia is bigger, but it counts as a continent.

Q: Mount Fuji is in which country?
A: Japan
X: China | South Korea | Nepal
F: Mount Fuji is a volcano. It last erupted in 1707.

Q: Which ocean lies between Europe and North America?
A: Atlantic Ocean
X: Pacific Ocean | Indian Ocean | Arctic Ocean
F: The Atlantic gets a few centimetres wider every year.

Q: What is the line around the middle of the Earth called?
A: The Equator
X: The Prime Meridian | The Arctic Circle | The Date Line
F: Days and nights are about the same length all year at the Equator.

Q: Where do orangutans live in the wild?
A: Borneo and Sumatra
X: Africa | South America | India
F: Orangutan means "person of the forest" in Malay.

Q: Mount Everest is in which range of mountains?
A: The Himalayas
X: The Alps | The Andes | The Rockies
F: The Himalayas are still growing, by about a centimetre a year.

Q: Which is the longest range of mountains on land?
A: The Andes
X: The Himalayas | The Rockies | The Alps
F: The Andes run for about 7,000 kilometres down South America.

Q: Which sea is so salty that people float in it easily?
A: The Dead Sea
X: The Red Sea | The Black Sea | The North Sea
F: The shore of the Dead Sea is the lowest dry land on Earth.

Q: The kiwi, a bird that cannot fly, comes from which country?
A: New Zealand
X: Australia | Fiji | South Africa
F: New Zealanders are nicknamed Kiwis after the bird.

[hard]
Q: Which is the biggest desert of all, counting the cold ones?
A: Antarctica
X: The Sahara | The Gobi | The Arabian Desert
F: Antarctica gets less rain and snow than the Sahara does.

Q: What is the tallest waterfall in the world?
A: Angel Falls
X: Niagara Falls | Victoria Falls | Iguazu Falls
F: Angel Falls in Venezuela drops 979 metres, three times the Eiffel Tower.

Q: What is the deepest place in the ocean?
A: The Mariana Trench
X: The Java Trench | The Tonga Trench | The Puerto Rico Trench
F: It is nearly 11 kilometres deep. Mount Everest would vanish in it.

Q: Which is the biggest lake in the world?
A: The Caspian Sea
X: Lake Superior | Lake Victoria | Lake Baikal
F: It is called a sea, but land surrounds it on every side.

Q: Which is the deepest lake in the world?
A: Lake Baikal
X: Lake Tanganyika | Lake Superior | Lake Titicaca
F: Lake Baikal in Russia holds about a fifth of the fresh water in all lakes and rivers.

Q: Which is the longest river in Europe?
A: The Volga
X: The Danube | The Rhine | The Thames
F: The Volga flows across Russia into the Caspian Sea.

Q: Which is the longest river in Asia?
A: The Yangtze
X: The Ganges | The Mekong | The Indus
F: The Yangtze flows 6,300 kilometres across China.

Q: Which country has the most active volcanoes?
A: Indonesia
X: Japan | Iceland | Italy
F: Indonesia lies on the Ring of Fire around the Pacific Ocean.

Q: Which desert in South America is one of the driest places on Earth?
A: The Atacama
X: The Sahara | The Gobi | The Kalahari
F: Parts of the Atacama in Chile have gone for years without a drop of rain.

Q: Where do Komodo dragons live in the wild?
A: Indonesia
X: Australia | India | Madagascar
F: The Komodo dragon is the biggest lizard in the world, up to 3 metres long.

Q: What is the highest mountain in South America?
A: Aconcagua
X: Kilimanjaro | Denali | Mont Blanc
F: Aconcagua in Argentina is the highest mountain outside Asia.

Q: Denali is the highest mountain on which continent?
A: North America
X: South America | Asia | Europe
F: Denali is in Alaska. Its name means "the high one".

Q: Which is the biggest lake in Africa?
A: Lake Victoria
X: Lake Tanganyika | Lake Malawi | Lake Chad
F: Lake Victoria is shared by Uganda, Kenya and Tanzania.

Q: Which strait separates Asia from North America?
A: The Bering Strait
X: The Strait of Gibraltar | The Strait of Hormuz | The Strait of Magellan
F: At its narrowest the Bering Strait is only about 85 kilometres wide.

Q: Which river made the Grand Canyon?
A: The Colorado
X: The Mississippi | The Rio Grande | The Missouri
F: The river took millions of years to cut a canyon more than a kilometre deep.

Q: Which is the biggest island in the Mediterranean Sea?
A: Sicily
X: Sardinia | Cyprus | Crete
F: Mount Etna on Sicily is one of the most active volcanoes in the world.

Q: The Galapagos Islands, home of giant tortoises, belong to which country?
A: Ecuador
X: Peru | Chile | Mexico
F: Galapago is an old Spanish word for tortoise.
